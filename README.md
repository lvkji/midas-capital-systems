## Title
Midas Capital Systems
## Description
Midas Capital Systems is a virtual paper-trading platform that simulates real time stock execution, portfolio tracking, and market data feeds 
## Motivation
Learning how to invest can be difficult without risk of financial loss because you must invest real money into the market to see a return; I tried finding a paper trading platform that I could use to see what would happen if I had a certain amount of money, but the good ones are all behind paywalls. Midas Capital Systems is the free tool you can use to learn how to invest in a brokerage without risk of financial loss!
## Quick Start
1.) Navigate to https://midas-capital-systems-mkkc5kykcyfr6ceukzkqne.streamlit.app/ and press "Yes! Get this app back up!"
2.) Make an account and choose the starting capital and begin buying/selling stock!
